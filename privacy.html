Privacy Policy

App name: SANDY (working name) · Last updated: October 21, 2026 (draft)

This policy explains, in plain words, what SANDY (the "app") collects and what we do with it. The app is run by Minds Above Matters LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: mindsabovemattersllc@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (used to sign in with a one-time link), an optional display name, and your time zone.
  • Your content: calendars, events, to-dos, categories, notes, checklist ticks and links you add.
  • Technical: basic logs kept by our hosting providers (for example IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. The app also stores some preferences (like your last view) on your device.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your calendars, sync them across your devices, and send sign-in emails.
  • To keep it secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

Who processes it for us

We use service providers that host or deliver the app: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub Pages (web hosting), an email delivery service for sign-in links, and OpenStreetMap-based search for location suggestions (the text you type in the Location field is sent to that service). If optional AI features are added later, this policy will be updated first to explain what is shared and with whom.

Who can see your data

Each account can only see its own calendars and notes; this is enforced by the database. Limited people at Minds Above Matters LLC may access data when needed to operate, secure or support the app, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Export: Menu → Account → Export my data downloads everything in your account as a file.
  • Delete: Menu → Account → Delete my account permanently deletes your account and all of its content. Copies in provider backups may remain for a limited time before they expire.
  • You can email us to ask what we hold about you or to correct it.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected with per-account access rules. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users if we learn of a breach involving their data, as required by law.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Where data is stored

Data is stored by our providers, mainly in the United States.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email.
