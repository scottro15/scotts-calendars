      ✨ What Sandy can do▾
      • Leave by: events with a location get a leave-by time in Heads Up, from home or from where you are. Push when it’s time to leave: Leave-by alerts, above.
      • Free gaps: to-do suggestions you can tap to schedule.
      • Clashes: Heads Up flags overlaps with shifts or practices.
      • Sundays: a week-ahead review.
      • Long day: after 5 PM, a card when tomorrow spans 10+ hours (or 8+ scheduled).
      • Tomorrow: after 6 PM, your first event, when to leave and what to prep.
      • Practice prep: before a Maryville or Jennings practice, your notes from the last one.
      • Travel plan: away and neutral Maryville games get when to leave, when you’ll be back, and the weather.
